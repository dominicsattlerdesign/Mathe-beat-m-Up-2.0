# ENGLISH (en-US)

## App name (max. 30 characters)
Mathbrawl – Math & Fight

## Short description (max. 80 characters)
Solve math problems and let your ninja fighter strike!

## Full description (max. 4000 characters)
Solve, Fight, Win!

In Mathbrawl you battle with ninjas, pirates and wizards in 3D arenas. Your fighter only attacks when you get the answer right. The faster you solve, the harder you hit.

THREE ATTACKS, THREE KINDS OF PROBLEMS
• Punch: addition and subtraction
• Kick: multiplication and division
• Throw: brackets, missing numbers, number patterns and symbol puzzles
• Special attacks and slow-motion finishers

FOUR DIFFICULTY LEVELS
• Grade 1–2: addition and subtraction up to 20
• Easy: times tables
• Medium: times tables, brackets and first steps in algebra
• Hard: big numbers up to 1000

VERSUS: TWO PLAYERS, ONE TABLET
• Two players solve against each other, one at the bottom, one at the top
• Each picks 2 fighters, best of 3 rounds
• Both right at the same moment? Then the fighters clash!

SO MUCH TO DISCOVER
• 15 fighters, each with their own punches, kicks, throws and special attacks
• 120 unique moves with fire, lightning, playing cards, cannonballs and more
• Story mode with 10 battles up to the Mathemagus
• Free fight against an opponent of your choice
• 10 fighters, second outfits, bonus arenas and music to unlock
• 30 entrances before the fight
• Leaderboard on your device

MADE FOR KIDS
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• No account, no chat, no data collection
• Works completely offline
• For phones and tablets, in English and German

Game idea & game design: Dominic Sattler. Programmed with Claude Code by Anthropic.
